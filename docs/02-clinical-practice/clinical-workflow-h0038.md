# Clinical Workflow & Medi-Cal H0038 Encounter Documentation

**Document Version:** 1.0-GA  
**Target Domain:** peernotebook.com  
**Billing Code:** HCPCS H0038 (Self-Help / Peer Support Services)  
**Standard:** California DHCS CalAIM Payment Reform & CMS 8-Minute Rule

---

## 1. Medi-Cal H0038 Overview
Procedure code **H0038** reimburses non-clinical peer support services provided by Certified Peer Support Specialists (CPSS). In California, under CalAIM, H0038 is billed in **15-minute increments** adhering strictly to the CMS 8-Minute Rule:

| Direct Face-to-Face Minutes | Billable Units |
|---|---|
| Less than 8 minutes | 0 units (Non-billable) |
| 8 – 22 minutes | 1 unit |
| 23 – 37 minutes | 2 units |
| 38 – 52 minutes | 3 units |
| 53 – 67 minutes | 4 units |
| 68 – 82 minutes | 5 units |
| 83 – 97 minutes | 6 units |

## 2. The Standard 4-Part Peer Note Anatomy
To pass county behavioral health and state Medi-Cal audits, every locked peer note must contain four distinct, non-clinical elements:

### Section 1: Recovery Plan Goal Addressed
* **Clinical Purpose:** Ties the encounter directly to the client's individualized care plan or wellness recovery action plan (WRAP).
* **Compliance Check:** Must state a specific client-chosen goal (e.g. permanent supportive housing, employment readiness, reducing substance use, building natural social supports).
* **Sample Entry:** *"Client focused on Goal 2 of their Wellness Recovery Action Plan: navigating supportive housing applications and attending landlord open house."*

### Section 2: Peer Support Intervention Provided
* **Clinical Purpose:** Proves that an authorized peer service was performed by the specialist.
* **Compliance Check:** Must specify concrete actions taken (e.g. active listening, strategic sharing of lived experience, role-playing interviews, providing warm handoffs).
* **Sample Entry:** *"Specialist shared lived experience regarding overcoming housing denial anxiety; reviewed housing voucher packet item-by-item; practiced role-playing apartment viewing questions."*

### Section 3: Peer Engagement & Client Response
* **Clinical Purpose:** Documents the peer's level of receptivity, emotional affect, and personal empowerment.
* **Compliance Check:** Must include at least one direct quote reflecting client voice and agency.
* **Sample Entry:** *"Client actively participated throughout the 45-minute session and expressed relief. Client stated: 'Having you walk through these forms makes me feel like I can actually get my own place again without getting overwhelmed.'"*

### Section 4: Next Steps & Plan for Next Contact
* **Clinical Purpose:** Ensures continuity of care and establishes when the next encounter will take place.
* **Compliance Check:** Concrete date, location, and action item for the specialist or client.
* **Sample Entry:** *"Specialist will accompany client to the county housing authority on Tuesday, Oct 28 at 10:00 AM. Client agreed to gather proof of income."*

## 3. 3-Way Time Tracking Requirement
In community outreach (Place of Service 15: Mobile/Field), travel time and documentation time are tracked alongside direct encounter minutes:
1. **Direct Minutes:** Face-to-face or telehealth time spent with the peer client.
2. **Travel Minutes:** Time required to travel to/from the field encounter location.
3. **Documentation Minutes:** Time spent preparing, recording, and locking the peer note.

Peer Notebook automatically computes total billable H0038 units based on Direct Minutes while recording Travel and Doc time for county administrative cost allocation.
