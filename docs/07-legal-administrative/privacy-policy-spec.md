# Privacy Policy Specification & HIPAA Privacy Practices

**Document Version:** 1.0-GA  
**Target Domain:** peernotebook.com  
**Applicable Statutes:** HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR Part 160 & Part 164) / California CMIA (Civil Code § 56) / California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

---

## 1. Core Privacy Commitments
At Peer Notebook, we honor the fundamental human right to privacy, dignity, and autonomy for both peer specialists and the vulnerable community members they support:

1. **Zero Data Monetization:** We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade peer client data, encounter records, or specialist telemetry under any circumstances.
2. **Minimal Data Collection:** We collect only the data strictly required to maintain audit-ready Medi-Cal H0038 billing compliance.
3. **Client De-Identification Support:** The platform supports client alias identifiers (e.g. initials or county case numbers) to avoid storing full legal names on mobile devices when appropriate.
4. **Offline Local Sovereignty:** Data stored in local device IndexedDB remains within the specialist's physical device until synchronized with authorized agency systems.

---

## 2. Permitted Uses & Disclosures of Health Information
In compliance with HIPAA and California CMIA, health information recorded in Peer Notebook is disclosed exclusively for:
* **Treatment & Peer Support Coordination:** Facilitating non-clinical peer support and warm handoffs.
* **Supervisory Review:** Authorizing clinical supervisors to verify and cosign encounter notes within 72 hours.
* **Health Care Operations & Medi-Cal Billing:** Generating validated claims manifests for submission to county behavioral health plans.
* **Mandatory Legal Exceptions:** Preventing imminent danger to self or others, or reporting child/elder abuse as legally mandated.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion Rights
* **Retention Period:** Medi-Cal encounter records are retained for a minimum of **10 years** in compliance with California Department of Health Care Services audit retention mandates.
* **Right to Request Restriction / Export:** Specialists and agencies can export complete JSON or PDF data dumps at any time without vendor lock-in.
