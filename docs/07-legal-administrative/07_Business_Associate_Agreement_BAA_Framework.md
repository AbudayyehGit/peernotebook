## 1. Executive Summary & Regulatory Purpose

Under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (**HIPAA**), Public Law 104-191, as amended by the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health (**HITECH**) Act, and the Omnibus Rule of 2013, **Peer Notebook LLC** functions as a **Business Associate (BA)** when providing its software platform, data synchronization, and documentation utilities to Covered Entities (including County Departments of Mental Health, Behavioral Health Plans, Federally Qualified Health Centers [FQHCs], and community-based organizations [CBOs]).

This institutional framework defines the standard operating procedures, contractual guarantees, subprocessor requirements, and security controls enforced by Peer Notebook LLC to safeguard electronic Protected Health Information (**ePHI**) generated by Certified Peer Support Specialists (CPSS-CA) in mobile community environments.

### Subprocessor Compliance Matrix:

1. **Supabase, Inc. (Database & Auth Tier):**
   * Role: Cloud relational storage, Row-Level Security policy enforcement, and authentication tokens.
   * Encryption: Disk-level AES-256 with customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK); TLS 1.3 in transit.
   * Agreement: Standard Enterprise BAA executed under 45 CFR § 164.502(e) and § 164.504(e).

2. **Amazon Web Services (Backup & Cold Storage Tier):**
   * Role: Automated cryptographic WAL archiving and encrypted database disaster recovery snapshots.
   * Compliance: AWS Artifact HIPAA BAA active; Write-Once-Read-Many (WORM) S3 Object Locking enabled.
   * Residency: Exclusively hosted within AWS US-West (Northern California / Oregon) regions.

3. **Vercel Global Edge Network (Frontend Distribution):**
   * Role: Stateless delivery of pre-compiled static assets (`dist/index.html`, JavaScript, CSS).
   * PHI Storing Policy: **Zero PHI at the Edge.** All client encounter data remains strictly within client-side IndexedDB or passes directly via encrypted HTTPS/WSS to Supabase without edge server logging.

4. **Zero-AI / Zero-Log Data Policy:**
   * Peer Notebook strictly forbids the use of public, non-BAA covered third-party AI chatbots or generative text synthesis tools that ingest client session notes.
   * Any server-side proxy integration maintains a strict **zero-data-retention, zero-model-training policy**. Encounter text is never parsed for machine learning model training.

## 3. Obligations and Activities of Peer Notebook LLC (Business Associate)

1. **Permitted Uses & Disclosures:**
   Peer Notebook LLC shall not use or disclose ePHI other than as permitted or required by the underlying software licensing agreement, this BAA, or as required by law. All disclosures shall be limited to the **Minimum Necessary** standard under 45 CFR § 164.502(b).

2. **Appropriate Safeguards (45 CFR § 164.308, § 164.310, § 164.312):**
   Peer Notebook LLC implements and maintains comprehensive administrative, physical, and technical safeguards:
   * **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Restricts data access strictly to authenticated specialists and designated supervisors within the contracting organization’s UUID boundary.
   * **Field Device Session Lock:** Mobile web sessions enforce client-side inactivity lockouts after 30 minutes.
   * **Local Storage Protection:** IndexedDB stores client records within browser origin sandbox boundaries; sensitive note drafts are sealed via browser WebCrypto primitives.
   * **Cryptographic Tamper Sealing:** Finalized session notes compute a SHA-256 cryptographic digest before transmission.

3. **Breach Notification Protocol (45 CFR § 164.410):**
   * In the event of an confirmed acquisition, access, use, or disclosure of unencrypted ePHI in violation of the HIPAA Privacy Rule:
   * **Immediate Assessment:** The Security Incident Team initiates immediate containment within 1 hour.
   * **Written Notice to Covered Entity:** Peer Notebook LLC shall notify the contracting Covered Entity in writing without unreasonable delay, and in no case later than **48 hours** following discovery.
   * **Contents of Report:** Notification shall include the identification of each individual whose ePHI has been breached, the nature of the data involved, unauthorized recipient details, and immediate mitigation steps implemented.

4. **Access and Accounting of Disclosures (45 CFR § 164.524, § 164.528):**
   * All mutations, exports, and supervisor reviews are recorded immutably in `audit_trail_logs`.
   * Specialists and covered entities can query and export complete accounting logs for audit inquiries within 5 business days.

## 4. Obligations of Contracting Covered Entities

1. **Specialist Credential Verification:** The Covered Entity represents and warrants that all users provisioned with specialist accounts are Certified Peer Support Specialists (CPSS-CA), registered peer trainees, or authorized clinical supervisors operating within legal scope of practice under California SB 803.
2. **Device Security Policy:** Covered Entity shall enforce mobile device management (MDM) or device password/biometric passcodes on all agency-issued tablets and smartphones utilized in field outreach (Place of Service 15).
3. **Appropriate Consent:** Covered Entity is responsible for securing any individualized digital or written client consents required under California Civil Code § 56 (CMIA) or 42 CFR Part 2 prior to recording substance use recovery encounters.

## 5. Term and Termination

1. **Term:** The institutional BAA framework becomes effective upon the execution of a Master Services Agreement or Enterprise Pilot Agreement with the Covered Entity and continues until all ePHI is returned or destroyed.
2. **Termination for Cause:** Either party may terminate the agreement immediately if the other party breaches a material term of this BAA and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of receiving written notice.
3. **Return or Destruction of ePHI:**
   * Upon termination, Peer Notebook LLC shall, if feasible, return or destroy all ePHI maintained in any form, including database tables and backups.
   * If return or destruction is infeasible due to statutory Medi-Cal 10-year audit record-keeping requirements under California DHCS regulations, Peer Notebook LLC shall extend the protections of this BAA to such ePHI and limit further uses and disclosures strictly to audit defense.
