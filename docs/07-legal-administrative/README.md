## Executive Overview
The **Legal & Administrative** suite establishes the corporate entity structure, intellectual property ownership chain of title, statutory healthcare compliance frameworks, client privacy specifications, terms of service, open-source attributions, and regulatory audit crosswalks that govern **Peer Notebook** (peernotebook.com) and protect the community organizations utilizing it.
