## 1. Parties & Purpose

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **Peer Notebook LLC** or a subscribing Community-Based Organization partner ("Agency") and the undersigned **Certified Peer Support Specialist** or Independent Contractor ("Contractor").

WHEREAS, Agency operates a behavioral health peer support program providing non-clinical services under California CalAIM and Medi-Cal HCPCS H0038 guidelines; and

WHEREAS, Contractor is a Certified Peer Support Specialist (CPSS-CA) in good standing under California Senate Bill 803 (SB 803) or Peer Recovery Coach (CPRC) possessing specialized lived experience and credentialing, operating an independent peer support consulting practice;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

---

## 2. Independent Contractor Status (California AB 5 & Labor Code § 2775)

1. **Freedom from Control:** Contractor maintains complete freedom from control and direction in the performance of services, both under contract and in fact, subject only to adherence to the California SB 803 non-clinical scope of practice and ethical standards.
2. **Independent Business:** Contractor customarily engages in an independently established trade, occupation, or business of the same nature as that involved in the work performed.
3. **No Employment Benefits:** Contractor acknowledges they are an independent 1099 contractor, not a W-2 employee, and are solely responsible for self-employment taxes (FICA, Medicare, federal/state income tax withholdings), health insurance, workers' compensation, and vehicle liability insurance.
4. **Mileage & Equipment:** In-field travel for Place of Service (POS) 15 encounter logging may be reimbursed at the standard IRS business mileage rate ($0.67 per mile) as agreed in specific work orders. Contractor provides their own mobile field devices (smartphones, tablets, or laptops).

---

## 3. Scope of Services & CalAIM Non-Clinical Boundaries

1. **HCPCS Code H0038 Services:** Contractor delivers non-clinical peer support services billable under HCPCS code H0038 in 15-minute increments according to CMS 8-minute rounding rules.
2. **Golden Thread Note Standard:** Contractor documents all sessions in the Peer Notebook application utilizing the 4-part non-clinical encounter format:
   * Linkage to client's self-directed recovery goal.
   * Specific non-clinical peer intervention (tagged with SB 803 core competencies).
   * Direct observation of client response with verbatim quotes (`"..."`).
   * Collaborative next steps and follow-up plan.
3. **Strict Non-Clinical Scope:** Contractor covenants never to engage in clinical diagnosis, psychotherapeutic treatment, crisis psychiatric triage, or medication management. Contractor adheres to DHCS Code of Ethics boundaries at all times.

---

## 4. Compensation & Billing Terms

1. **Compensation Rate:** Agency shall compensate Contractor at the agreed hourly rate or per-unit rate ($32.50 per 15-minute unit county benchmark allocation as specified in Task Order).
2. **Encounter Verification:** Payments are disbursed based on verified, cosigned session notes recorded in Peer Notebook with valid SHA-256 cryptographic audit logs.
3. **Submission Timelines:** Notes must be submitted within 24 hours of encounter completion to allow timely clinical supervisor attestation.

---

## 5. HIPAA, CMIA & Confidentiality Safeguards

1. **Business Associate Obligations:** Contractor agrees to comply with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164, and the California Confidentiality of Medical Information Act (CMIA).
2. **De-Identification & Zero-Trust Field Use:** Contractor shall utilize Peer Notebook's dynamic client de-identification toggle ("Client #A849") when working in public or semi-public field locations.
3. **Breach Notification:** Contractor shall notify Agency and Peer Notebook Security Officer within twenty-four (24) hours of discovering any potential unauthorized acquisition or disclosure of protected health information.
