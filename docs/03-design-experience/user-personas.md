# Empirical User Personas & Behavioral Friction Profiles

**Document Version:** 1.0-GA  
**Target Domain:** peernotebook.com

---

## Persona 1: Elena Martinez, CPSS-CA (Field Peer Support Specialist)
* **Background:** 4 years in sustained recovery from substance use disorder; Certified Peer Support Specialist working for a community harm reduction non-profit in Los Angeles County.
* **Environment:** 80% mobile field outreach (encampments, drop-in centers, transitional housing, bus stops). Uses an agency-issued 10-inch Android tablet with intermittent 4G LTE.
* **Core Pain Points:**
  * Has lost notes three times due to sudden browser refresh when cellular signal dropped under highway overpasses.
  * Experiences severe cognitive fatigue at the end of shifts trying to recall exact encounter start and stop times.
  * Stresses over audits because traditional EHRs demand clinical jargon she wasn't trained to write.
* **How Peer Notebook Solves Her Day:**
  * IndexedDB guarantees notes are saved immediately to local tablet storage.
  * 4-part guided structure prompts her to capture verbatim client quotes in under 2 minutes.
  * Visual green badges assure her that her note meets CalAIM H0038 billing requirements before she leaves the client.

## Persona 2: Marcus Vance, CPRC (Peer Recovery Coach)
* **Background:** Lived experience with justice involvement and mental health recovery; provides 1-on-1 recovery coaching and employment readiness mentorship.
* **Environment:** Hybrid (hospital crisis stabilization unit and community college libraries).
* **Core Pain Points:**
  * Needs to capture travel time and direct face-to-face time separately to justify billing.
  * Wants clean, professional PDF exports to give to clients or attach to county portal uploads.
* **How Peer Notebook Solves His Day:**
  * Direct, travel, and doc time inputs automatically calculate 15-minute billing units without mental math.
  * One-click PDF generation creates a tamper-evident, SHA-256 sealed document with supervisor signature blanks.

## Persona 3: Sarah Jenkins, LCSW (Behavioral Health Supervisor)
* **Background:** Licensed Clinical Social Worker supervising a team of 8 Peer Support Specialists at a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC).
* **Environment:** Desktop office workstation and weekly supervision meetings.
* **Core Pain Points:**
  * Spends hours rejecting peer notes that fail county Medi-Cal audits because they missed a goal reference or direct quote.
  * Fears retroactive Medicare/Medicaid audit clawbacks.
* **How Peer Notebook Solves Her Day:**
  * Pre-lock automated validation prevents specialists from submitting incomplete notes.
  * Review queue allows rapid cosigning with cryptographic timestamp logs.
