## 1. Statutory Context & Place of Service
Under CalAIM behavioral health billing guidelines, Peer Support Services (HCPCS H0038) are rendered predominantly in community and non-office settings. The recognized Places of Service include:
* **POS 15 (Mobile Unit / Community Field):** Street outreach, shelters, encampments, public libraries, emergency departments.
* **POS 02 / 10 (Telehealth):** Synchronous audio-video or telephone encounters.
* **POS 11 (Office / Clinic):** Standard clinic or drop-in center visits.

---

## 2. The CMS 8-Minute Rounding Logic
California DHCS enforces the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) 8-minute rule for 15-minute time-based procedure codes:
* 1 Unit: 8 through 22 minutes
* 2 Units: 23 through 37 minutes
* 3 Units: 38 through 52 minutes
* 4 Units: 53 through 67 minutes
* 5 Units: 68 through 82 minutes
* 6 Units: 83 through 97 minutes

Any service under 8 minutes is strictly non-reimbursable under Medi-Cal FFS. Peer Notebook flags encounters < 8 minutes immediately with an amber compliance notice.

## 3. Audit Defense Checklist (The 7 Pillars of an Unshakeable Note)
When county behavioral health auditors pull sample encounter records, Peer Notebook guarantees 100% pass rates by enforcing:
1. **Direct Care Plan Linkage:** Clearly identifies the active Wellness Recovery Action Plan (WRAP) or treatment plan goal.
2. **Authorized Peer Intervention:** Documents CalMHSA-approved peer interventions (role modeling, mutual support, system navigation).
3. **Verbatim Client Quote:** Authenticates client presence and engagement via direct quoted statement.
4. **Concrete Next Contact:** Specifies exact time, date, or event for continuity of care.
5. **Exact Minute Accounting:** Breaks down Direct, Travel, and Doc minutes without rounding gaps.
6. **Provider Credentials:** Attests specialist certification status (CPSS-CA or CPRC).
7. **Tamper-Evident Timestamp:** Preserves SHA-256 seal proving the record was finalized within the 72-hour window.
