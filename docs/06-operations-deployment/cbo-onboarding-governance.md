## 1. Overview
Community-Based Organizations (CBOs) and peer-run respites require clear, audit-proof administrative oversight to bill Medi-Cal H0038 services under CalAIM contracts.

The Peer Notebook **CBO Administrative Dashboard (`/admin/dashboard`)** provides:
1. **Agency Credentialing & County Provider Profile:**
   - County Code selector (Alameda, Los Angeles, San Francisco, San Diego, Sacramento, Contra Costa, etc.)
   - Organization NPI (Type 2 Facility)
   - Medicaid Provider ID
   - Contract Classification (CBO Partner, Prime Medi-Cal Contractor, County Subcontractor)
   - Allocated Monthly Billable Units Ceiling
2. **Guided 6-Step Medi-Cal Provider Onboarding Checklist:**
   - County Mental Health Plan Contract Execution
   - National Provider Identifier (NPI-2) Verification
   - Clinical Supervisor Cosignature Hierarchy Configuration
   - CalMHSA Peer Specialist Scope & Code of Ethics Attestation
   - Direct EHR / ANSI X12 837P Batch Billing Interface Setup
   - Business Associate Agreement (BAA) Execution
3. **Specialist & Supervisor Roster:**
   - 1099 Independent Contractor vs. W-2 Employee Classification
   - Hourly Rate ($35–$65/hr benchmark) and IRS Mileage Reimbursement ($0.67/mile)
   - CPSS-CA Certification and Licensure tracking
4. **CalAIM Monthly Volume & Capacity Tracker:**
   - Real-time aggregation of billable units ($32.50/unit benchmark)
   - Direct face-to-face, travel, and documentation time distributions
   - Capacity percentage tracking against monthly county contract limits
5. **Direct EHR & ANSI X12 837P Batch Export:**
   - Generates Medi-Cal ready batch claims export for agency billing teams.
