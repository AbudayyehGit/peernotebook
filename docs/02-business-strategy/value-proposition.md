# Value Proposition Canvas & ROI Metrics

**Document Version:** 1.0-GA  
**Target Domain:** peernotebook.com

---

## 1. Customer Pains, Pains Relieved & Measurable Gains

### Peer Specialist Pains
* **After-Hours Documentation Burnout:** Spending 2–3 hours at home typing clinical notes into clunky desktop EHR portals.
* **Fear of Audit Denials:** Uncertainty over whether non-clinical notes satisfy Medi-Cal CalAIM compliance.
* **Loss of Unsaved Notes:** Losing extensive session records when cell service drops in shelters or rural visits.

### Peer Notebook Relievers
* **Sub-3 Minute Note Completion:** Guided 4-part prompts with CalMHSA quick-select pills drastically cut typing fatigue.
* **Automated CalAIM Validation:** Immediate green check indicators confirm notes meet Medi-Cal H0038 requirements before locking.
* **100% Offline IndexedDB Persistence:** Notes write immediately to device storage with zero reliance on cloud connectivity.
