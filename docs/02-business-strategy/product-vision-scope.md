# Product Vision & Scope: Non-Clinical Peer Recovery Ethos

**Document Version:** 1.0-GA  
**Target Domain:** peernotebook.com  
**Ethos:** Non-Clinical, Strengths-Based, Trauma-Informed Lived Experience

---

## 1. Product Vision Statement
To empower Certified Peer Support Specialists and Peer Recovery Coaches with a specialized, field-resilient tool that reduces note-logging anxiety from hours to minutes, safeguards audit compliance under California CalAIM, and honors the human dignity of peers through strengths-based, non-clinical language.

---

## 2. Core Pillars of Non-Clinical Peer Practice
Unlike licensed therapists or psychiatrists whose notes center on pathology, symptom severity, and diagnostic classification (DSM-5), Peer Support operates on fundamental principles of:

1. **Mutuality & Lived Experience:** Peers share personal recovery journeys strategically to inspire hope, foster psychological safety, and reduce isolation.
2. **Strengths-Based Language:** Documentation focuses on client assets, self-advocacy, personal agency, and natural community supports rather than deficits.
3. **Trauma-Informed Design:** Both the software interface (low arousal, soft slate, zero pop-up alarm fatigue) and the note structure avoid clinical retraumatization.
4. **Non-Judgmental Harm Reduction:** Meeting peers wherever they are in their recovery journey without punitive documentation requirements.

---

## 3. Product Scope: In-Scope vs Out-of-Scope

### In-Scope (GA #8 Release)
* **Standard 4-Part Encounter Note:**
  1. Personal Recovery Plan Goal Addressed
  2. Peer Support Intervention Provided (mapped to CalMHSA competencies)
  3. Peer Engagement & Direct Client Quotes
  4. Next Steps & Plan for Next Contact
* **3-Way Travel, Direct, and Documentation Time Tracker:** Automated 15-minute unit calculations.
* **Offline-First PWA:** Guaranteed offline note capture with zero data loss.
* **Supervisor Review Queue & Cryptographic Locking:** Tamper-evident locking with SHA-256 seals.

### Out-of-Scope
* Clinical diagnostic formulation tools.
* Prescribing or medication management modules.
* Generic chatbots or automated generative AI note synthesis.
