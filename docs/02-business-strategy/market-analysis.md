# Behavioral Health Market Analysis & CalAIM Catalysts

**Document Version:** 1.0-GA  
**Target Domain:** peernotebook.com  
**Macro Trends:** California CalAIM, Medicaid Section 1115 Waivers, SB 803 Certification

---

## 1. Market Sizing (TAM / SAM / SOM)
* **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** National Medicaid Peer Support & Community Health Worker software market ($820M across 48 states reimbursing peer services).
* **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM):** California Medi-Cal Specialty Mental Health & Substance Use Disorder (SUD) Peer Specialist organizations ($145M across 58 county behavioral health plans).
* **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):** Initial 3-Year focus on California CBOs, non-profit community health clinics, and independent certified specialists ($18M).

---

## 2. Regulatory Tailwinds
1. **California SB 803:** Standardized statewide peer specialist certification through CalMHSA, establishing recognized billing codes.
2. **CalAIM Enhanced Care Management (ECM):** In-person community engagement requires real-time mobile documentation in non-traditional settings.
3. **Behavioral Health Payment Reform:** Transition from Cost-Based Reimbursement to Fee-For-Service (FFS) schedules puts pressure on agencies to accurately bill H0038 15-minute units.
