## 1. SaaS Pricing Architecture

### Tier 1: Solo Peer Specialist (Free Forever / Community Edition)
* **Price:** $0 / month
* **Target:** Independent Certified Peer Support Specialists and Recovery Coaches.
* **Included Features:**
  * Complete 100% offline IndexedDB note logging.
  * Automatic CalAIM H0038 15-minute billing unit calculator.
  * PDF note export with SHA-256 cryptographic seal.
  * JSON backup and restore data portability.
  * Local storage of up to 25 active peer clients.

### Tier 2: Community Team Tier
* **Price:** $29 / specialist / month (billed annually)
* **Target:** Non-profit behavioral health CBOs with 2–15 peer workers.
* **Included Features:**
  * Unlimited peer client records.
  * Secure Supabase PostgreSQL cloud sync with multi-device replication.
  * Supervisor review queue with electronic cosigning.
  * Exportable agency audit log CSVs.
  * Dedicated email support and onboarding guide.

### Tier 3: County Agency & Enterprise Health System
* **Price:** Custom Enterprise Agreement ($45–$65 / seat / month + implementation)
* **Target:** County Departments of Mental Health (e.g. LACDMH, SFDPH) and large health networks.
* **Included Features:**
  * Custom EHR / HL7 FHIR bi-directional interface.
  * Single Sign-On (SSO) with Okta / Azure Active Directory.
  * Dedicated PostgreSQL cluster with Custom Business Associate Agreement (BAA).
  * 99.9% uptime SLA and dedicated compliance engineer.
