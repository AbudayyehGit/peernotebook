# CalMHSA 17 Core Peer Competencies & Non-Clinical Practice Crosswalk

**Document Version:** 1.0-GA  
**Target Domain:** peernotebook.com  
**Certifying Authority:** California Mental Health Services Authority (CalMHSA)  
**Legislation:** California Senate Bill 803 (Beall, Chapter 150, Statutes of 2020)

---

## 1. Statutory Context
Under California SB 803, Certified Peer Support Specialists (CPSS) must be certified through CalMHSA-approved training programs demonstrating proficiency across **17 Core Competencies**. Peer Notebook integrates these competencies directly into interactive intervention tags within the encounter logging interface.

---

## 3. Scope of Practice Safeguards
Peer Notebook enforces strict boundary warnings:
* **Prohibited Terms Filter:** Warns if users enter diagnostic terminology (e.g. "schizophrenic decompensation", "borderline acting out", "incompatible with therapy").
* **Mandatory Strengths Prompts:** Encourages replacement with recovery-oriented phrasing (e.g. "Client navigating severe anxiety; expressed desire to rest in quiet space").
