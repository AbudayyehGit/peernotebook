**Document Version:** 2.0-GA  
**Target Domain:** peernotebook.com  
**Certifying Authority:** California Mental Health Services Authority (CalMHSA)  
**Legislation:** California Senate Bill 803 (Beall, Chapter 150, Statutes of 2020)  
**Reimbursement Standard:** DHCS CalAIM Behavioral Health Payment Reform (HCPCS H0038)

---

## 1. Statutory Context & Legislative Mandate
Under California Senate Bill 803, Certified Peer Support Specialists (CPSS-CA) must be certified through CalMHSA-approved training programs demonstrating proficiency across **17 Core Competencies**. Peer Notebook integrates these competencies directly into interactive intervention tags, documentation prompts, and GoalPath coverage metrics within the encounter logging interface.

Under CalAIM Behavioral Health Payment Reform, peer support services are recognized as Medicaid-reimbursable services under procedure code **H0038** (Self-Help / Peer Support Services) at 15-minute billing increments ($32.50/unit benchmark).

## 3. Scope of Practice Safeguards & Clinical Jargon Cleanser
Peer Notebook enforces strict boundary warnings and non-clinical terminology rules:
* **Prohibited Clinical Jargon Filter:** Flags medicalized terminology (e.g., "decompensation", "non-compliant", "borderline", "delusional") and offers 1-tap replacements with recovery-oriented phrasing.
* **CalAIM 4-Part Structure:** Guarantees every encounter records (1) Goal Addressed, (2) Peer Intervention, (3) Client Response, and (4) Plan/Next Steps.
* **1099 vs. W-2 Classification:** Accommodates both agency staff and mobile independent contractors with travel mileage ($0.67/mi) and hourly billing ledgers.
* **Zero-AI Philosophy:** All notes and compliance tags are 100% human-crafted and specialist-owned, ensuring full audit defensibility before county health plans.
