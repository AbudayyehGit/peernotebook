## Executive Overview
The **Business Strategy & Clinical Practice** documentation suite articulates Peer Notebook's commercial thesis, clinical mission, non-clinical scope of practice, and unit economics. Peer Notebook serves the rapidly expanding community of over 5,000 Certified Peer Support Specialists across California, operating under Medi-Cal CalAIM payment reform.
