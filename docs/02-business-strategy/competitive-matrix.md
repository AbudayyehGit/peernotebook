## 2. Competitive Moat
* **Peer-Specific Linguistic Alignment:** Peer Notebook speaks the actual language of lived experience, eliminating clinical alienation.
* **Dual-Layer Field Resilience:** Flawless transition between online and zero-cell environments without lost input.
* **CalAIM Exactitude:** Guaranteed Medi-Cal audit protection removes fear of retroactive clawbacks.
